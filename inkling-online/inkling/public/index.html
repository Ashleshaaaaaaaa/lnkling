About

Inkling is a free online multiplayer drawing and guessing game. A game has a few rounds. In every round each player draws a word they choose, while everyone else races to guess it and earn points. Whoever has the most points at the end wins.

What's inside

  • Public rooms and private rooms with an invite link
  • Up to 20 players, 28 languages, custom word lists
  • Normal, Hidden and Combination word modes
  • Undo, fill, eraser, 20 colors, left and right click colors
  • Hotkeys you can change, sound volume, mobile layout
  • Kick, ban, votekick, mute and report tools

How to play

  1. Pick a word. When it's your turn, choose a word to draw.
  2. Draw it. Sketch your word. No writing letters!
  3. Let them guess. Everyone else types guesses into the chat.
  4. Guess too. When someone else draws, race to type the answer.
  5. Win. The faster you guess, the more points you get. Most points wins!
